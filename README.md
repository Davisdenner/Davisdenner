## A little about me

I'm a **Data Scientist** with a degree in **Physics** and postgraduate training in **Data Science at PUC-Rio**. My background shapes the way I approach problems: start with the underlying assumptions, test ideas against data, and keep the result understandable to the people using it.

I work across the full analytical process, from preparing data and developing predictive models to designing interactive tools that help teams plan, monitor, and make decisions. My interests sit where **statistical reasoning, machine learning, and practical business questions** meet.

## How I approach data problems

**Understand the decision** → **Build a useful baseline** → **Validate what the model learns** → **Communicate uncertainty** → **Make the result usable**

I care about both technical rigor and practical adoption. A model is most valuable when its assumptions, limitations, and outputs are clear enough to guide a real decision.
